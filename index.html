03 / A practical Kakobuy shopping guide

From interesting find
to informed shortlist.

01

Choose the category

Start with shoes, a specific clothing layer, or an accessory. Comparing similar items makes differences easier to see.

02

Save the exact variant

Record the product URL, color, size, and material description. Different variants can have different prices and availability.

03

Separate facts from gaps

Mark missing measurements, unclear photos, and unconfirmed charges. Do not turn an unknown detail into an assumption.

04

Recheck before buying

Open the current listing again. Confirm the selected option, applicable terms, and available cost information.

04 / Keep the useful details

A better spreadsheet starts here.

Use these fields in your own research sheet. This is a comparison framework, not a live inventory or price feed.

Suggested fields for organizing Kakobuy finds
FieldWhat to recordWhy it helps
Product & sourceProduct name, direct listing URL, and category.Lets you return to the exact item rather than a similar search result.
Selected variantColor, size, model, and included pieces.Keeps different configurations from being treated as the same offer.
Fit & measurementsAvailable dimensions, measurement units, and sizing notes.Makes comparison more useful than relying on size letters alone.
Materials & detailsListed composition, construction details, and unresolved questions.Separates stated specifications from visual impressions.
Price & currencyCurrent variant price, currency, and date checked.Prevents an old or mismatched price from driving the decision.
Additional costsKnown delivery charges and fees; separately marked estimates.Shows which parts of the delivered total remain uncertain.
Evidence & termsRelevant photos, seller information, and applicable return conditions.Keeps supporting information available for a final review.

Budget framework: item price + applicable delivery + service charges + taxes or duties. Include only charges that apply; an estimate is not a checkout quote.

For shoes & clothing

Compare the seller’s measurement guidance with your own measurements or a garment that fits. Note whether dimensions refer to the body or the finished garment. For jerseys and sets, check each included piece rather than assuming every component fits the same way.

For headwear & jewelry

Record circumference, adjustability, chain length, ring size, and material information where available. For Kakobuy accessories, close-up photos can clarify shape and finish, but cannot independently confirm composition or authenticity.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is a browsing and comparison guide. Its category buttons open CAFANS category pages, and its general browsing buttons open the CAFANS homepage.

How should I compare shoe and clothing finds?

Compare the exact selected variant, available measurements, material description, current price, and return conditions. For shoes, check the seller’s sizing guidance and foot-length measurements. For clothing, compare garment measurements with an item that already fits.

Does a listed price represent the final delivered cost?

Not necessarily. A product price may exclude domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, taxes, or duties. Record confirmed charges separately and keep estimates clearly marked.

Do brand names or product photos prove authenticity?

No. Brand names and photographs alone do not establish authenticity, seller authorization, or current availability. Request appropriate product documentation and evaluate the seller independently.
